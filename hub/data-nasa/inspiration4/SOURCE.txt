Blood panels of the four SpaceX Inspiration4 private astronauts (subjects C001-C004),
3-day flight, September 2021. Published by NASA's Open Science Data Repository (OSDR).

  LSDS-8_Comprehensive_Metabolic_Panel_CMP_TRANSFORMED.csv
      OSD-575 (Inspiration4 plasma/serum), metabolic panel, 19 analytes. Analyser: Beckman Coulter AU5800.
  LSDS-7_Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_TRANSFORMED.csv
      OSD-569 (Whole Blood Measurements from the SpaceX Inspiration4 Mission), complete blood count, 20 analytes.

Listing:  https://osdr.nasa.gov/osdr/data/osd/files/569 and https://osdr.nasa.gov/osdr/data/osd/files/575
Metadata: https://osdr.nasa.gov/osdr/data/osd/meta/575
Files:    https://osdr.nasa.gov/geode-py/ws/studies/<OSD-n>/download?source=datamanager&file=<name>
Downloaded 14 Nov 2026, no login. Copied here unchanged (same SHA-1 as the download).
Public release date (OSD-575 metadata): 14 May 2024.

Wide format: one row per sample ("C001_serum_L-3", "C001_whole-blood_L-3_cbc"), then for each
analyte its value and the lab's own reference range (min, max), the unit in the column name.
28 samples per file: 4 people x 7 days. Days are counted from launch (L-92, L-44, L-3) and from
return (R+1, R+45, R+82, R+194). NONE of the samples was taken in flight.
Example: C001 glucose 69 mg/dL at L-3, lab range 65-99; sodium 142 mmol/L, lab range 135-146.

What is not verified:
- Hemoglobin is filed as "percent". Its values (11.5 to 17.0) and its ranges (13.2-17.1 and
  11.7-15.5) read as g/dL, and each is about a third of the same sample's hematocrit. Astrum keeps
  the file's unit and leaves hemoglobin out of the demo until the original lab report is checked.
- C003's lab ranges for hemoglobin and hematocrit change between L-3 (13.2-17.1, 38.5-50) and
  R+1 (11.7-15.5, 35-45). The file does not say why.
- A few cells are blank, "NA" or "N/A" (BUN/creatinine ratio, total bilirubin, eGFR maximum, and
  MCH, MCHC, MCV for some samples). Astrum leaves those out.
- The lab's ranges are the lab's, not the medical lead's. Astrum flags results only against
  her blood.json.
- Terms: no licence string in the study metadata. OSDR's FAQ and terms ask that datasets be cited
  with the study page's citation button and that OSDR be credited
  (https://science.nasa.gov/reference/osdr-faq, https://science.nasa.gov/reference/osdr-help-terms-and-conditions).
  The full terms text was not read.

Astrum never edits these values. In demo mode it plays one subject's values as one simulated
crewmate's blood results, marked "replayed", each naming the subject and the day.
