The ruleThe chip sends only what a part measured. Every value that did not come from this board's own sensors is made by the hub, in demo mode only, and carries its label on screen.
When a part is addedIts driver goes into the firmware and the reading leaves the missing list. The hub switches to the measured value by itself; nothing else changes.
